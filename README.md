# The Final Shift (TFS)

A 15-floor office platformer that runs in the browser. You play a night-shift IT tech who climbs Synergex Tower, beats two mini bosses and the CEO, and uploads the loophole that ends the shift for good.

After the ending:

- **Floors** opens for practice, with a ghost of your best run and Normal / Hard / Extreme difficulty.
- **Boss Mode** (next to the Floors title) is a gauntlet of every boss. Clear Easy, Hard and Extreme to unlock **Impossible** (35 bosses).
- **Infinite Levels** is an endless Annex with a mini boss every 5th floor and a boss every 10th.
- Extra avatars unlock. Beating Impossible earns the title **Dan The I.T. Man** and opens the **leaderboard** (saved on your device).

## Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. Nothing to install.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → or A D | Move |
| Space / W / ↑ | Jump (hold for higher) |
| S / ↓ | Drop through desks |
| F | Throw IT gear |
| R | Restart the current floor |
| T | Show or hide the stopwatch |
| P / Esc | Pause |

Progress, best times and ghosts are saved in your browser.
